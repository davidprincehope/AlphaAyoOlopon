# Ayo play interface

## Register

product

## Users

Players using the local browser adapter to play Ayo Olopon against the project's
trained AlphaZero checkpoint, with mouse, touch, or a keyboard.

## Product Purpose

Make a match feel like sitting at an Ayo board. The rules engine and trained
opponent remain authoritative; the interface makes turns, sowing, captures,
and outcomes easy to follow.

## Brand Personality

Tactile, focused, considered. The user chose a wooden Ayo board and a focused
match interface as the direction for this redesign.

## Anti-references

The old orange notebook viewer, crowded animation controls, and unnecessary
arcade effects. The board should not compete with controls or explanatory text.

## Design Principles

- Give the physical board the most attention.
- Show whose turn it is and how close each player is to winning.
- Put match actions within easy reach; disclose playback tools when requested.
- Preserve clear pit counts, legal moves, both seats, undo, and authoritative replay.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve keyboard shortcuts and visible focus, text descriptions of state,
announced turns, seed counts independent of seed graphics, and reduced-motion
support. Keep the board usable on narrow touch screens.
