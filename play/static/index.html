    How to play

    Choose a lit pit in your bottom row. Its seeds are sown counter-clockwise, one in each pit. If the last seed lands in an occupied pit that is not captured, pick up that pit and keep sowing.

    Capture four

    A pit reaching four while seeds remain in hand is captured by that row’s owner. Four made by the final seed belongs to the player making the move.

    Keep the game moving

    If your opponent’s row is empty, feed it when possible. When play cannot continue, a position repeats, or the move limit is reached, remaining seeds are collected by row.

    Capture 25 seeds to win. 24 each is a draw.
